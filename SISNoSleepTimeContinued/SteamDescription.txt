[h2]What is this?[/h2]
This mod just disables sleep needs from the game. By the default behaviour, only your community has their sleep disabled, but you can change this in the settings.

[b]Can be added to current saves.[/b]

[b]Not tested in coop.[/b]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
The settings file can be found in the mod's folder under "Settings_NoSleepTime.xml".
[list]
[*] EnabledForAnimals. Whether animals will have their sleep needs deactivated. (default false)
[*] EnabledForNonPlayableCharacters. Whether characters (humans) from other communities will have their sleep needs deactivated. (default false). 
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Shouldn't have any incompatibilities. Report any issues in the comments section!
